# Privacy Policy

**Version 1.0.0 — Effective 2 June 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how the **Contextual Contacts** application
("the App", "we", "us") handles your information. By using the App you agree to
the practices described below.

## 1. Who we are

Contextual Contacts is published by Codelio. For any privacy-related question
you can reach us at **contact@codelio.fr**.

## 2. Information we process

The App is **offline-first**: your data lives on your device unless you choose
to enable cloud sync. Depending on the features you use, the App processes:

- **Contact data you create or import** — names, phone numbers, email
  addresses, notes, tags, relationship strength, the date and place you met a
  person, and the connections between contacts.
- **Device contacts** — only when you explicitly grant the Contacts permission
  to import them. Import happens on demand; we do not read your contacts in the
  background.
- **Location data** — when you open the Location view and grant permission, the
  App reads your current GPS position to centre the map on you. This position is
  used only to render the map and is **not stored or transmitted**.
- **Account data** — if you sign in to enable cloud sync, we process a user
  identifier and (for email sign-in) your email address through Firebase
  Authentication.

## 3. How we use your information

We use the information above solely to:

- store and display your contacts and the relationships between them;
- place contacts on a map and timeline;
- synchronise and back up your data to the cloud **when you enable that
  feature**;
- send you local reminders to reach out to contacts (these are generated on your
  device).

We do **not** sell your data, and we do **not** use it for advertising or
profiling.

## 4. Cloud sync and storage

Cloud sync and cloud backups are **opt-in**. When enabled:

- Your data is stored in **Google Firebase / Cloud Firestore** and associated
  with your authenticated account.
- Authentication is handled by **Firebase Authentication**.
- Data in transit is encrypted using TLS, and Google encrypts data at rest.

If you never sign in, no contact data leaves your device through the App.

## 5. Third-party services

The App relies on the following processors:

- **Google Firebase** (Authentication, Cloud Firestore) — account and synced
  contact data. See Google's privacy policy at
  <https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy>.
- **Apple Maps** (via MapKit) — map tiles for the Location view. Map usage is
  governed by Apple's privacy policy.

## 6. Data retention and deletion

- Local data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App.
- Synced data remains in Firestore until you delete it from within the App or
  request deletion.
- To request deletion of any cloud-stored data, email **contact@codelio.fr**.

## 7. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including the EU/EEA under the GDPR), you may
have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to
withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, contact **contact@codelio.fr**.

## 8. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
data from them.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version and
effective date are shown at the top of this document. Material changes will be
reflected by a new version number.

## 10. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Email us at **contact@codelio.fr**.
