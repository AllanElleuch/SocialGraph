# SocialGraph — Suno Song #2

A funky, playful hip-hop/funk promo track for **SocialGraph** (by Codelio) — a
different vibe from the pop anthem. Groovy, confident, a little tongue-in-cheek.

Paste the **Style** into Suno's *Style of Music* box and the **Lyrics** into the
*Lyrics* box. Title suggestion: **"Keep In Touch (Don't Ghost Me)"**.

---

## Song Description (Simple mode — let Suno write the lyrics)

Use this when you want Suno to generate the lyrics itself. Switch Suno to
**Simple** mode and paste this whole paragraph into the *Song Description* box
(no separate style/lyrics fields needed).

```
A funky, playful funk-hop / nu-disco track (around 105 BPM) to advertise
"SocialGraph", a phone app that maps your whole social circle as a living graph
of glowing nodes. Slap bass, wah-wah guitar, brass stabs, tight boom-bap drums
and retro Rhodes keys. Confident, charismatic, laid-back rap verses with a
soulful sung hook and call-and-response gang vocals. The vibe is cheeky and
tongue-in-cheek: a person buried in two thousand contacts, half of them ghosts,
finally takes control. They pull up the graph, search and tap-to-call in a
second, merge duplicate contacts, get nudged to text friends before six weeks of
silence, rack up streaks, badges and stats for staying social, and keep
everything safely synced in the cloud. The closer the friend, the brighter the
connection glows. The hook is "Keep in touch, don't ghost me — your people are
the dopest", and it ends on the tagline "who's in your orbit?". Party energy,
groovy, fun, radio-ready.
```
