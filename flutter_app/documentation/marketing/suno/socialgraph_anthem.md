# SocialGraph — Suno Song

A fun, upbeat promo anthem to present **SocialGraph** (by Codelio): the app that
turns your messy contacts into a living map of your relationships — search,
tap-to-call, reminders, stats, achievements, and a beautiful social graph.

Paste the **Style** into Suno's *Style of Music* box and the **Lyrics** into the
*Lyrics* box. Title suggestion: **"Who's In Your Orbit"**.

---

## Style (paste into "Style of Music")

```
Upbeat indie-pop / electro-pop, 120 BPM, bright synth plucks, punchy four-on-the-floor
kick, handclaps, sparkly arpeggios, warm bassline, big sing-along chorus. Modern,
playful, tech-startup energy — think a phone-ad anthem. Male+female duet vocals,
catchy hooks, gang vocals on the chorus. Clean, radio-ready, feel-good.
```

## Title

```
Who's In Your Orbit
```

## Notes & variations

- **Shorter ad cut (~30s):** use *Intro → Pre-Chorus → Chorus → Outro* only.
- **Genre swaps to try in the Style box:** `synthwave / retro 80s`, `acoustic
  folk-pop ukulele`, `lo-fi hip-hop chill`, or `epic cinematic trailer pop`.
- **Feature callouts you can re-lyricize per video scene:** the social graph view,
  search + tap-to-call/text, duplicate merge, stay-in-touch reminders, stats &
  achievements, cloud sync & backup, relationship strength.
- Keep the hook **"Who's in your orbit?"** consistent across cuts — it doubles as
  a tagline.
