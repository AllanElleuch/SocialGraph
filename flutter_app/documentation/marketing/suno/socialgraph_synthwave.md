# SocialGraph — Suno Song #3

A nostalgic 80s synthwave / retrowave promo track for **SocialGraph** (by
Codelio) — neon, cinematic, and emotional. A different lane from the pop anthem
and the funk-hop cut.

Paste the **Style** into Suno's *Style of Music* box and the **Lyrics** into the
*Lyrics* box. Title suggestion: **"Neon Constellations"**.

---

## Song Description (Simple mode — let Suno write the lyrics)

Use this when you want Suno to generate the lyrics itself. Switch Suno to
**Simple** mode and paste this whole paragraph into the *Song Description* box.

```
A nostalgic 80s synthwave / retrowave anthem (around 110 BPM) to advertise
"SocialGraph", a phone app that turns your contacts into a glowing neon map of
the people in your life. Lush analog synth pads, gated reverb drums, arpeggiated
bass, shimmering DX7 bells and a soaring lead synth — cinematic, emotional,
driving-at-night energy. Dreamy male or female vocals with big reverb and a huge
euphoric chorus. The song follows a person who feels scattered and out of touch
under a sky of forgotten names; then the app lights everything up like neon
constellations. They reconnect with one tap, get gentle reminders before
friendships fade, watch their closest bonds glow the brightest, and feel their
whole circle pull back together — all safely backed up in the cloud. The chorus
soars on the hook "light up the night, who's in your orbit?". Builds to an
uplifting, goosebumps final chorus. Retro, neon, heartfelt, radio-ready.
```
